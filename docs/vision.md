# Vision and intent

## Vision

Wayfare should become a private memory and decision layer between social discovery and real travel.

It is not intended to replace maps, booking sites, or social networks. It should connect the useful fragments they produce, preserve the original evidence, and help two people make a decision that fits their circumstances.

## Product promise

**Never lose a place that might become a meaningful trip.**

Saving should be immediate. Understanding can happen later. Planning should use what the couple has already collected rather than begin from an empty search box.

## Intended experience

At discovery time:

- Share the content to `Save to Wayfare`.
- Receive immediate confirmation that it was preserved.
- Do not require a long form or a planning decision.

At review time:

- See the original source and captured evidence.
- Confirm or correct uncertain details.
- Add a personal rating, note, or category.

At decision time:

- Choose an origin, budget, available time, and preferences.
- See plausible destinations ranked transparently.
- Understand why each place is recommended and which information may be stale.

After travel:

- Mark the destination visited.
- Record dates, actual cost, impressions, and whether it should be revisited.
- Improve future recommendations using lived experience rather than engagement signals.

## Design principles

### Capture first, organize later

The system should tolerate incomplete input. Losing a place is worse than creating an inbox item that needs review.

### Honest uncertainty

An extracted claim is not automatically a fact. Each important field can be marked as verified, extracted, estimated, stale, or missing.

### Couple-aware, not socially performative

Preferences belong to each person. The product should help find mutual choices without turning travel into a public feed or competition.

### Personal and portable

The users should be able to export their complete collection, including structured data and source references.

### Useful without AI

OCR, rules, and manual correction should produce a viable product. Local AI may reduce effort, but disabling it must not break capture, browsing, or planning.

### Free means safely free

The experiment must not depend on trials, payment cards, metered services with accidental charges, or an assumed future subscription.

## What Wayfare is not

- A booking engine
- A public travel social network
- A live price-comparison service
- A general-purpose bookmark manager
- An autonomous travel agent
- A replacement for turn-by-turn navigation
